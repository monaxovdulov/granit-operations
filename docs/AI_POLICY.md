# AI Policy

Status: disabled for S01

AI replies are not part of S01.

Deferred AI surfaces:

- website widget AI;
- Telegram AI;
- Mastra/OpenAI workflows;
- prompts and tool schemas;
- model config;
- eval/regression gates for AI behavior;
- urgent AI routing.

Later AI policy must enforce:

- approved AI disclosure before normal AI conversation;
- `from X` starting-price orientation only from approved sources;
- no final price, final deadline, warranty, contract, discount, availability, legal, payment, or similar promises;
- refusal/handoff for inheritance, burial, legal funeral questions, and other non-monument topics;
- deterministic tools for business truth;
- safe fallback on model/tool failure;
- send-time takeover gate.

Do not enable AI replies until later accepted slices provide persistence, lifecycle, takeover, policy, observability, and eval evidence.
